* [S8 Lev Manovich — Understanding Hybrid Media（2007）](s8-manovich-hybrid-media.md) - Manovich：深度可混合性——混合的不只是内容，还有技法、工作方法与表现方式。

* [R16 RED — Shutter Angles & Creative Control](r16-red-shutter-angle.md) - RED 技术文章：快门角度定义、曝光时间、帧间运动痕迹及画面速度条件。
* [R17 Marc Levoy — CS178, Noise and ISO](r17-levoy-noise-iso.md) - 斯坦福 CS178 讲义：光子／读出噪声、信噪关系与 ISO 增益。
* [R18 Marc Levoy — CS178, Color II: Applications in Photography](r18-levoy-color.md) - 斯坦福 CS178 讲义：白平衡、色度与摄影颜色呈现。
* [R19 John M. Henderson & Taylor R. Hayes — Meaning-based guidance of attention in scenes as revealed by meaning maps](r19-henderson-hayes-meaning-maps.md) - Nature Human Behaviour 2017 观看实验：意义图与显著性图比较，场景语义对眼动分布有重要解释作用。
* [R20 ／ S9 Loschky, Larson, Magliano & Smith — What Would Jaws Do? The Tyranny of Film and the Relationship between Gaze and Higher-Level Narrative Film Comprehension](r20-loschky-what-would-jaws-do.md) - PLOS ONE 2015 电影观看实验：前文语境显著影响理解与预测，而眼动仍高度同步——看见同一处≠理解相同。
